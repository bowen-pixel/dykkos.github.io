01 / Neural networks & graph search

WikiClick

Finding paths through Wikipedia.

A Python project combining directed hyperlink graphs, BFS reference paths, semantic features, and neural distance and next-link ranking experiments. I use PyTorch and NetworkX to explore how learned navigation compares with simpler search heuristics.

Saved-graph experiments and live navigation are separate: live routes are checked against Wikipedia links, but aren't guaranteed to be globally shortest.

Solidity & ETH payment channels

Educational smart contracts with companion notes, tests, and local-chain demos. The project moves from storage and coin ledgers to simple and long-lived ETH payment channels, including typed signatures, escrow accounting, replay protection, and withdrawal behavior.

A learning lab with reference implementations and a self-review; the contracts have not been independently audited.
